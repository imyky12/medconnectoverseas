# Privacy Policy

At MedConnectsOverseas ("MCO"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, register for conferences, workshops, mentorship programs, or other events, make a booking, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using MedConnectsOverseas services or submitting your information to us, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with MedConnectsOverseas:

### Personal Information

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone/WhatsApp number
- University, college, organization, or institution name
- Country/city of residence where relevant
- Academic or professional information where required for a particular program
- Any other information voluntarily provided during registration

### Registration & Event Information

- Events, conferences, workshops, or programs you register for
- Ticket or registration details
- Attendance information
- Workshop/session preferences
- Certificate-related information
- Information provided for participation in specific educational or practical activities

### Payment & Transaction Information

- Payment method
- Transaction/reference number
- Payment amount and date
- Relevant payment or bank transaction details required to verify your registration

MedConnectsOverseas does not operate its own payment gateway and does not intentionally collect or store your full credit/debit card number, CVV, PIN, or online banking credentials. Payments may be made through bank transfers, payment links, authorized third-party payment providers, or other payment methods designated by MCO.

Where a third-party payment provider is used, your payment information may be processed according to that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

### Technical Information

When you access our website, we may automatically receive certain technical information, including:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device type
- Operating system
- Website usage information
- Cookies and similar technologies
- Pages visited and general interaction data

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

- Process and confirm event, conference, workshop, or program registrations.
- Verify payments and registration status.
- Send confirmation emails, WhatsApp messages, SMS, phone calls, reminders, and event-related updates.
- Provide event information, schedules, venue details, and important announcements.
- Issue certificates, participation records, or other event documentation.
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
- Manage attendance and event access.
- Organize workshops, practical sessions, and other educational activities.
- Improve our website, programs, events, and participant experience.
- Conduct internal administration, record keeping, and analytics.
- Prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorized registrations, or other activities that may harm MCO or its participants.
- Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or governmental requirements.
- Send promotional or marketing communications where permitted by applicable law or where you have provided appropriate consent.

## 3. Sharing of Information

MedConnectsOverseas does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited personal information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

### Event Partners & Venues

We may share relevant registration information with event partners, speakers, trainers, institutions, venues, or authorized event personnel when necessary to facilitate event participation, entry, certificates, workshops, or other event-related services.

### Payment & Financial Service Providers

Where payment verification or processing involves a third-party payment provider, bank, or financial institution, relevant transaction information may be shared or processed as necessary to complete or verify the transaction.

### Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers for services such as website hosting, registration management, email communication, messaging, ticketing, analytics, cloud storage, or other operational functions.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal information where required by applicable law, regulation, court order, governmental authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of MCO, our participants, or others.

## 4. Data Security

MedConnectsOverseas takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission, internet communication, or digital storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored electronically.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of any passwords, access credentials, or other account information associated with your use of our services.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

- Managing event and program registrations;
- Maintaining attendance and certificate records;
- Resolving disputes or registration-related issues;
- Maintaining financial and transaction records;
- Meeting legal, regulatory, accounting, or administrative requirements; and
- Protecting our legitimate business interests.

You may request deletion of your personal information where legally permitted. However, certain information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate organizational purposes.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information held by MCO about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of your personal information where legally permitted.
- Request restriction of certain processing activities where applicable.
- Withdraw consent for certain communications or processing activities where consent is the legal basis.
- Opt out of promotional communications at any time.

You may continue to receive essential communications related to registrations, payments, event changes, safety information, certificates, or other necessary administrative matters even after opting out of promotional communications.
